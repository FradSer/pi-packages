Add an output-silence stall watchdog for resident teammates. A teammate wedged mid-turn (for example, blocked forever in a provider request) never accumulates assistant turns, so turn budgets could not fire and the roster showed "working" indefinitely while steering writes silently vanished into an unread pipe. The harness poll now tracks `lastOutputAt` per teammate: after 30 minutes without any RPC output (`PI_TEAMMATE_STALL_NOTICE_MS`, 0 disables) the leader receives one actionable notice per silence episode naming the teammate and recovery options; after 2 hours of total silence (`PI_TEAMMATE_STALL_SHUTDOWN_MS`, 0 disables) the child is reclaimed through the normal bounded shutdown path with the reason surfaced in the leader report. Any stream activity or prompt delivery re-arms the watchdog. Steering a silent working teammate now warns that delivery is uncertain instead of claiming success, and the widget, console roster, and detail views show how long a working teammate has been silent.
