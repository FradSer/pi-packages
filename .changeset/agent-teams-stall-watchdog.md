Two-tier stall watchdog: a working teammate silent since spawn with zero lifetime model output and no running tool — the provider-hang signature — is flagged after five minutes (`PI_TEAMMATE_SILENT_STALL_MS`, 0 disables) instead of the general thirty-minute window. Stall notices carry silence duration, spawn age, and lifetime token/cost diagnostics, and the zero-output notice names shutdown plus respawn as the effective remedy since steering cannot unstick an in-flight request. Streaming usage is now persisted to the roster, making zero-token hangs visible before shutdown.
